Cats

The cat (Felis Catus), also called domestic cat and house cat, is a small domesticated carnivorous mammal. It is a member of Felidae family, the domestic cat is the only domesticated species of the family Felidae.

Distribution of domestic cats worldwide

the domestic cat occurs across the globe, and is valued by humans for companionship and its ability to kill vermin. it is commonly kept as pet, working cat, and pedigreed cat shown at cat fancy events. out of the estimated 600million domestic cats worldwide:

  • 400 million reside in Asia, including 58 million in China
  • about 73.8 million cats are estimated to reside in the US
  • and about 10.9 million are in the United Kingdom

Naming

the origin of englih name cat,old english catt,is thought to be late latin word cattus, which was first used at the beginning of the sixth century,the late latin name may be derived from unidentified African language. the Nubian word kaddiska 'wild cat' and Nobiin kadis are the possible sources.

Domestication

it was long thought that the domestication of cats began in ancient Egypt, where cats were veneratd from around 3100 BCE.

Diet

cat is an obligate carnivore and has adapted to low fiber highly digestable meat diet. cats have fewer enzymes capable of metabolising carbohydrates compared to omnivores.

Senses

  1. Vision:

    cats have excellent night vision and can see at one sixth the light level required for human vision

  2. hearing:

    the domestic cat's hearing is most acute. it can detect an extremely broad range of frequencies ranging from 55 Hz to 79 KHz, whereas humans can only detect frequencies between 20 Hz and 20 KHz.

  3. Smell:

    cats have an acute sense of smell due in part to their well developed olfactory bulb, and large surface of olfactory mucosa, which is about twice that of humans.

  4. Taste:

    cats have 470 taste buds, compared to more than 9000 taste buds on humans tongue. cats have no the ability to taste sweetness, but they do taste acids.

Whiskers

To aid with navigation and sensation, cats have dozens of movable whiskers (vibrissae) over their body, most prominently on their faces, as seen by the long, white lines diverging outwards from its nose. These provide information on the width of gaps and on the location of objects in the dark, both by touching objects directly and by sensing air currents. They trigger protective blink reflexes to protect the eyes from damage.

hunting

Cats hunt small prey, primarily birds and rodents,and are often used as a form of pest control,Other common small creatures, such as lizards, snakes and insects, may also become prey. Cats use two hunting strategies, either stalking prey actively, or waiting in ambush until a prey animal comes close enough to be captured
